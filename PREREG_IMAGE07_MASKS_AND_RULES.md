collision, and extraction result. The raw Stage-F null expectation is
`11,207,042,464 / 2^32 = 2.6093` fold collisions; Stage G's expectation is its
actual generated count divided by `2^32`. Collisions and uniqueness are not
clues. Only duplicate successful, hash-identical extraction recovers the key.
A miss closes only these exact families; no post-hoc mutation or mask extension.
